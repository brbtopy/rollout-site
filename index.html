What Roll Out is

Roll Out is a marketplace that connects people who need a vehicle towed with nearby independent tow operators. You set your pickup and destination, get matched with a driver, and follow the truck on its way.

Tows are paid in cash directly to the driver on completion. Roll Out is the technology that makes the match and the communication — the towing itself is provided by the independent operator.
