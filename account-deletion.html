Delete your account & data

You can delete your RollOut account and its data at any time. Here's how, and what happens next.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open the RollOut app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Personal info.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm by typing DELETE.

Your account is deactivated right away and you're signed out. It stays recoverable for 30 days — just sign back in during that time to restore it — and is then permanently deleted.

Or request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email devs.rollout@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll verify it's you and complete the deletion, typically within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile (name, email, phone), your saved places, and your trip history associated with your account. This happens 30 days after you ask — the account is recoverable until then — after which it is permanently erased.

We keep a de-identified record of completed tows, containing nothing that identifies you, so the other party's own history stays accurate. Some records may be retained only where required by law (for example, to comply with legal or safety obligations), and are deleted once those obligations end.
