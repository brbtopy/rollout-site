Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026

Your privacy matters. This is the plain version of what we collect and why.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what RollOut collects, why, and your choices. It applies to the RollOut app and services.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email, phone number, role (rider or driver), and an optional profile photo.
  • Location information: your device location to set pickups, match you with nearby drivers, and show the truck en route. When a driver goes online, we also collect their location in the background so they keep receiving nearby jobs while the app is closed or the screen is off.
  • Trip information: pickup and drop-off addresses, vehicle/truck type, status, and timestamps.
  • Driver information: for drivers, vehicle details, photos of your driver's licence (front and back), its number and expiry, and a photo of your truck — which our team reviews by hand to approve you.
  • Device and usage information: basic app, device, and diagnostic data, including a push notification token and in-app activity such as screen views and key actions (see “Analytics and diagnostics” below), to keep the Service working, send trip updates, improve reliability, and stay secure.

How we use your location

As a rider, we use your location only while you are arranging or on a trip — to place your pickup, find nearby drivers, and show live progress. We do not collect a rider's location in the background.

As a driver, when you choose to go online we collect your location in the background — even when the app is closed or the screen is off — so you keep receiving nearby jobs. This runs only while you are online, a standing notification shows while it is active, and it stops the moment you go offline. We ask for this permission separately and explain it in the app before it starts.

You can control location access in your device settings, but the core matching features need it to work.

How we use information

To provide and operate the Service, match riders and drivers, let a rider call their driver by phone during a trip, keep accounts secure, comply with law, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

  • With your driver or rider: the details needed to complete the tow. A rider sees their driver's name, photo, and phone number (so they can call them) and the truck's live location on the way; a driver sees the pickup and drop-off to reach you.
  • With service providers who run the platform on our behalf — for example hosting and database (Supabase), maps and routing (Mapbox), push notification delivery (Expo and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging), crash, error and performance monitoring (Sentry), and product analytics (PostHog). They may process data only to provide their service to us. We review driver documents ourselves rather than sending them to an outside identity-verification service.
  • For legal reasons: where required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.

Analytics and diagnostics

To keep RollOut stable and to improve it, we collect information about how the app performs and how features are used — for example screen views and key actions like requesting a tow, getting a price, a driver accepting a job, and trips completing or being cancelled.

Crash and performance data is handled by Sentry, and product-usage analytics by PostHog. These events are tied only to your account identifier and your role (rider or driver) — never your name, phone number, email, or location. We use them to find and fix problems and to improve the Service, not to advertise to you.

Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

You can delete your account from the app. When you do, it is deactivated right away and scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days — signing back in during that window restores it. After 30 days your profile and personal records are permanently erased. We may keep a de-identified record of completed tows, containing nothing that identifies you, so the other party's own history stays accurate, and we may retain limited information where the law requires it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can review and update your profile, manage notification preferences, control location permissions in device settings, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your data; contact us to exercise them.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Data deletion

You can delete your account from within the app (Profile → Personal info → Delete account), or by requesting deletion at devs.rollout@gmail.com. Your account is deactivated right away and permanently deleted after 30 days, and you can restore it by signing back in during that window. See our account deletion page for details.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

Questions or requests? Contact devs.rollout@gmail.com.
